TeamCraft is a team chat for companies and organisations that run their own server. Channels, direct messages, calls and meetings are end-to-end encrypted: the server stores and forwards messages but can't read them.

You need a TeamCraft server to use the app. Your organisation runs it, and you sign in with your company account (single sign-on).

CHAT
• Channels in spaces, direct messages and group chats
• Threads, replies, reactions, mentions and @channel
• Edit, delete, forward, pin and save messages
• Photos and files with previews, sharing from other apps
• Message priority (important, urgent) and read confirmations
• Drafts, unread markers and jump to the first unread message
• Search with filters (person, channel, date, files, links)

CALLS AND MEETINGS
• Voice and video calls in direct messages and channels
• Join with the camera on or muted, speaker and video controls
• Privacy code to make sure nobody listens in
• Upcoming meetings and joining the meeting's call
• Calls ring like phone calls and work with Android Auto

ENCRYPTION AND PRIVACY
• End-to-end encryption with the MLS standard (RFC 9420)
• Every device has its own keys; link a new device by QR code
• Recovery by passphrase or through your organisation
• Security codes to verify contacts, warning when their keys change
• App lock with biometrics or screen lock, screenshot protection
• No ads, no tracking; push notifications don't carry message content

NOTIFICATIONS
• Push through Firebase or UnifiedPush
• Per-conversation notification levels, mute and do not disturb
• Reply and mark as read from the notification

FOR ORGANISATIONS
• Runs on your own server; open source (AGPL-3.0)
• Sign-in through your identity provider (OpenID Connect)
• Administrators manage people and channels without access to message content

TeamCraft is also available for Windows, macOS, Linux and the web. Source code, documentation and server setup: https://github.com/Zeitgleich/TeamCraft
