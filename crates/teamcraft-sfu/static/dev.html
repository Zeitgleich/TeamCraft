SFU audio test

Development page. Sends 20 ms Opus frames (WebCodecs) from the microphone or a test tone through the SFU as RTP datagrams with the audio level extension, and decodes and plays what arrives. Media is not DAVE-encrypted here. Open it in two tabs or browsers with different device ids.
